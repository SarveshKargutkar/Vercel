How it works

Each round you're paired up: one of you has the knowledge, the other has the controls. Talk it out — crack the password, walk the wire maze blind, defuse the bomb. But hidden saboteurs want your team to fail…

After every round: 30 seconds to discuss and vote a suspect. The chamber is loaded with live rounds and blanks — the suspect pulls the trigger. Blank = safe. Live = gone. Find a 🔍 magnifying glass to peek at the next shell.
